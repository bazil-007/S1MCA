Paradise Resort is a peaceful getaway located in Vattavada, Kerala, surrounded by the scenic beauty of the Western Ghats. The resort offers comfortable rooms, Wi-Fi, breakfast, room service, free parking, BBQ facilities, and hiking activities. Guests can explore nearby attractions such as Top Station, Pampadum Shola National Park, Kundala Lake, and Kundala Dam, making it an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.
